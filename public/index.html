Quick rules

  • Card points: A 11, 10 10, K 4, Q 3, J 2, 9 0. Reach 66 to win the deal.
  • Highest of the led suit wins; a trump beats any other suit.
  • Leading K or Q with its partner in hand (after you've won a trick) declares a marriage: +20, or +40 in trump.
  • Trump 9: swap with the face-up trump while leading.
  • Close the stock: no more drawing; you must reach 66 or the opponent scores 2 (3 if you took no tricks). Once closed or empty, you must follow suit, trump, and win if you can.
  • Reaching 66 scores 1 point; 2 if the opponent has under 33; 3 if they took no tricks.
  • If nobody gets 66, the last trick wins 1 point.
